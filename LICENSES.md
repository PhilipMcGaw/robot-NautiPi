for components, libraries, or imported material.

## Directory guidance

The `ROV - Conductivity Temperature and Depth Transducer/` and `ROV - Light Module/`
directories contain current project material for their respective hardware
modules. Check each project README and any source or imported-material notices
before modifying or redistributing content.

Third-party components, symbols, footprints, libraries, reference designs,
reference documents, and imported historical material may have separate terms.
Retain their copyright and licence notices, and do not imply that they are
covered by the project licences above.

Project-authored reference material is covered by CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 only where it
is clearly identified as project-authored. Material retained for reference but
authored by another party remains subject to its original terms.

This file is a project-maintenance guide, not legal advice. When distributing
assembled hardware or a combined product, review the obligations for every
included component and source.
