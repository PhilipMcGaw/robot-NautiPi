## Directory guidance

The `ROV - Conductivity Temperature and Depth Transducer/` and `ROV - Light Module/`
directories contain the current project material for their respective hardware
modules. Check each project README and any source or imported-material notices
before modifying or redistributing content.

Third-party components, symbols, footprints, libraries, reference designs,
and imported historical material may have separate terms. Retain their
copyright and licence notices, and do not imply that they are covered by the
project licences above.

This file is a project-maintenance guide, not legal advice. When distributing
assembled hardware or a combined product, review the obligations for every
included component and source.
